# p14 — SiteForge (건설 관련 앱, 실제 건설)

**Sovereign Vision**: Full app (not mini-app) for real-world construction management. Project bidding, site logging with voice, material procurement, payments in stablecoins, team coordination. Actual construction focus: building sites, timelines, safety, costs.

**Core Features**:
- Project dashboard: active builds, timelines, budgets.
- Bid/offer system: submit bids for projects, FOMO on deadlines.
- Site logs: p6 Voice reports (Lung Surprise Eye for "intensity" of issues), photos, notes.
- Material trade: integrate p13 trade + p10 stable payments (Harvest Credits).
- Team: p9 live for on-site meetings, p3 companions as AI foremen?
- ALWAYS LEARNING: Codex for project lessons, voice re-listen evolves "best practices".
- Da Vinci: SENSE beauty in UI (clean cards, sfumato progress bars), Vitruvian proportions in site plans, empirical observation.

**Legion Cross**:
- p6: Voice as primary for logs, claims, negotiations (surprise boosts priority or accuracy).
- p10: Stablecoin payments for materials/labor with fee framing.
- p13: Trade platform for bulk materials (cross p10 credits).
- p9: Live platform for virtual site tours or team sync.
- p11: Metaverse for 3D previews of builds?
- p12: Idea submission for new construction projects/innovations.
- p7: Errands for on-site tasks (delivery, cleanup).
- p1-p5: Gacha for material luck, magic for "optimizations", etc.

**Full-Cheat / Psych**:
- FOMO: limited bid windows, scarcity of prime projects.
- Variable ratio: random "bonus" on on-time completion.
- Near-miss: "Almost on schedule".
- Loss aversion: "Don't lose the build".
- Endowment: "Your site is yours".
- Sunk cost: volume of projects.
